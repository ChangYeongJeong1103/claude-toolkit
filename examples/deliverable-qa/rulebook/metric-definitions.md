# Metric Definitions

Owner: Measurement Science · Last updated: 2026-06-15

| Metric | Formula | Shown as |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Unique exposed households | integer |
| Frequency | Impressions ÷ Reach | 1 decimal |
| Tune-in rate | Tune-ins ÷ Reach × 100 | percent, 2 decimals |
| Cost per tune-in (CPT) | Spend ÷ Tune-ins | currency, 2 decimals |
| CPM | Spend ÷ Impressions × 1000 | currency, 2 decimals |
| Incremental lift | (Exposed rate − Control rate) ÷ Control rate × 100 | percent, 1 decimal |

Notes
- Compute from the base numbers, then round to the precision shown.
- Reach is deduplicated households; reach across platforms cannot be added together unless the report says it is deduplicated.
