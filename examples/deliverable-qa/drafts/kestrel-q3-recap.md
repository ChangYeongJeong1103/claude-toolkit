# Kestrel Studios — September Launch Recap

**Measurement period:** September 1, 2026 – September 30, 2026

The September launch campaign reached 3.05 million households across streaming and live TV. Streaming delivered the stronger conversion rate, while live TV delivered the lower cost per tune-in.

Households exposed to the campaign tuned in at 0.75%, versus 0.50% for the matched control group: an incremental lift of 50.0%.
