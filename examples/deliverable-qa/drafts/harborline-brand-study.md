# Harbor Line Auto — Q3 Brand Study

**Measurement period:** July 1, 2026 – September 30, 2026

## Highlights
Harborline's Q3 campaign delivered 3,000,000 impressions to 1,200,000 households (frequency 2.5) at a CPM of $24.00 on $72,000.00 of spend.

## Dealer-site visits
Exposed households visited a dealer site at a rate of 1.20%, versus 0.96% for the control group: an incremental lift of 25.0%. Households that recieve the ad more than three times were more likely to visit, which was observed across all regions.

## Methodology
Data source: ACR viewing panel matched to first-party site-visit data. Segments below 50 households are not reported.
